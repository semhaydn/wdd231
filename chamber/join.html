NP Membership Benefits

Designed for eligible non-profit organizations. Membership has no fee.

  • Basic member listing in the chamber directory.
  • Access to selected community networking events.
  • Chamber newsletters and public business updates.
  • Opportunities to share eligible community initiatives.

Bronze Membership Benefits

For businesses starting to build their chamber network.

  • Standard business listing in the member directory.
  • Access to member networking events.
  • Member newsletter and business resource updates.
  • Discounted admission to selected training sessions.

Silver Membership Benefits

For established businesses seeking stronger visibility.

  • Enhanced directory listing with business description and website link.
  • Discounted access to chamber workshops and luncheons.
  • One member business spotlight opportunity each year.
  • Priority registration for selected networking events.

Gold Membership Benefits

Premium visibility and access for organizations ready to lead.

  • Premium directory placement and expanded company profile.
  • Priority business spotlight and promotional opportunities.
  • Invitations to selected executive networking events.
  • Complimentary or discounted access to selected chamber training and luncheons.
  • Priority consideration for event sponsorship opportunities.
